# Prime OS Pitch Slides Plan

## Summary
Build 4 slides for CR/Masuda: PrimeOS = **commerce operating system + intelligence layer**, not another SaaS/agency/CRM/OMS tool. Source basis: [competitor research](/Users/thanhnguyenxuan/Documents/MyProject/PrimeOS/docs/08-competitor-research.md), [system map](/Users/thanhnguyenxuan/Documents/MyProject/PrimeOS/docs/02-prime-os-system-map.md), [Masuda pitch notes](/Users/thanhnguyenxuan/Documents/MyProject/PrimeOS/docs/07-meeting-summary-masuda-pitch.md). External check: Kibo, Fluent, Salesforce, Shopify official pages.

## Key Slides
**1. Competitive Advantage**
Headline: `PrimeOS connects execution depth with ecosystem intelligence.`  
Core advantages: closed-loop commerce data, COS execution core, AI with proof/approval/audit, CR transactional intelligence, Japan-to-global/cross-border wedge.  
2-axis chart:
- PrimeOS: `X 8.5 / Y 9`
- CRM / ERP: `X 6 / Y 6.5`
- Marketplace ops tools: `X 7.5 / Y 4.5`
- E-commerce agencies: `X 6.5 / Y 3.5`
- SaaS point tools: `X 3.5 / Y 3`

**2. Product Roadmap**
Two 3-month phases, all done in 6 months.
- Phase 1, Months 1-3: defensible MVP. Intelligence hub, Ecom/COS core, CRM lead/RFQ, Customer timeline, Finance evidence pack, one full demo loop.
- Phase 2, Months 4-6: commercial-ready loop. AI operator, CR workflow integration, multi-role surfaces, Standard/Pro packaging, SLA/support ops, paid pilot readiness.
End-state: `signal -> demand -> customer -> order -> finance readiness -> learning loop`.

**3. Revenue In Process With CR Global Team**
Frame as pipeline, not booked revenue unless CR gives numbers.  
Visual: funnel/lane from `CR network -> qualified sellers -> onboarding -> PrimeOS usage -> orders/GMV -> subscription + service + finance upside`.  
Revenue buckets: SaaS MRR, setup/onboarding fee, managed ops service, success/GMV fee, future finance referral/revenue share.

**4. Package Pricing**
Use 3 packages:
| Package | Target | Included | Limit | Service |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | SME / factory / single brand | Intelligence Lite, Ecom/COS core, CRM lead/RFQ, CRM Compact, basic Finance docs | 3-5 users, 2 channels, 500 SKUs | Email support, monthly review |
| Pro | agency / growth brand / CR operator team | Standard + AI operator, advanced analytics, retention, finance trust profile, integrations | 10-25 users, 5 channels, 5k SKUs | Priority support, weekly ops review |
| Enterprise / CR Ecosystem | CR global / multi-entity / partners | Pro + SSO, custom data, role workspaces, bank/partner views | Custom | Dedicated PM, SLA, custom rollout |

Directional pricing: Standard `$1.5k-3k/mo`, Pro `$4k-8k/mo`, Enterprise custom. Setup fee separate.

## Public Interface / Wording
No code/API change. Public wording must avoid overclaim:
- Say `funding readiness`, not loan approval.
- Say `transactional intelligence option`, not digital bank promise.
- Say `AI drafts/recommends with approval`, not autonomous execution.

## Test Plan
Acceptance per slide:
- Competitive slide answers “why PrimeOS wins.”
- Roadmap fits exactly 6 months.
- Revenue slide separates confirmed pipeline vs future upside.
- Pricing slide has target customer, modules, usage limit, service level.
- Story stays business-first, not architecture-heavy.

## Assumptions
- Audience = CR Global / Masuda.
- Revenue numbers unavailable, so placeholders or pipeline stages only.
- Pricing is directional USD; convert to JPY/VND only after target market lock.
